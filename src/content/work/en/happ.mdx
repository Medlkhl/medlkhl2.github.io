### Chapter one: from React Native to Xamarin (2021–2024)

HAPP started out on React Native. As Software Engineer, I led its strategic platform migration to Xamarin and realigned the app’s architecture with the company’s technology standards. The migration was completed successfully.

In the same role I designed and implemented integrations with internal enterprise services. They extended what the app can do and kept the communication between the systems reliable.

### Chapter two: leading HAPP’s development (2024–2025)

In July 2024 I took over the lead of HAPP’s development as Senior Software Engineer, with one goal: long-term stability and maintainability.

- **APIs and apps.** I built and maintained scalable ASP.NET REST APIs and integrated them into the cross-platform .NET MAUI and Xamarin apps, with smooth delivery to iOS and Android.
- **Security.** I closed the vulnerabilities found in security audits and implemented authentication and authorisation with Keycloak over OpenID Connect and OAuth2.
- **Architecture.** I refactored the codebase to MVVM and Clean Architecture, so presentation logic lives in view models and business rules stay independent of the UI framework and the infrastructure.
- **Delivery and support.** I administered and maintained the deployment environments and supported HAPP’s users with fault analysis and problem resolution.
- **People.** I established a code-review governance framework and coached the team through structured code reviews and 1:1 sessions.
