### The idea

A screen is flat, but we judge depth partly by how a scene shifts as we move our head. HoloBox tracks the viewer’s head and redraws the scene from exactly that viewpoint, so the avatar seems to stand inside a box behind the glass rather than on its surface.

### How it works

The app is a .NET MAUI Blazor Hybrid application. Its WebView runs Three.js for the avatar and MediaPipe for face tracking. The .NET side runs a Gemma model in-process through LLamaSharp, loaded from a GGUF file. Speech recognition turns what the viewer says into text, the model answers, and text-to-speech gives the avatar its voice, with lip-sync. [CONFIRM: speech and voice engines]

### Why everything stays local

Running the model inside the app keeps the whole conversation on the device. There is no network round trip, it works offline, and nothing the viewer says has to leave the machine. HoloBox is in development, Windows first.
